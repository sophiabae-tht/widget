Android Large 360×800

Healthy Spending Card on the widget

Two widget directions, built only with what a real Android home-screen widget can do. Tap a Healthy Spending Card tile to see the full list, then Back to see where it lives in Care hub.

Widget 1Flippable

  • Each purse shows its amount and “1 of 4”, one purse at a time. No total. The “1 of 4” stays put while the purses slide; only the number changes.
  • Tapping the bottom half, with the › in the corner, goes to the next balance: it slides in from the right as the current one slides out to the left. Where the incoming text reaches the wallet icon or the ›, it fades out instead of running under them. After the last purse it wraps to the first.
  • Tapping the top half opens the full list. The wallet icon in the top-right corner stays the same for every purse.
  • Purse names are the Nations portal names, shortened only where they don't fit two lines:
    • Health Screening (2026 Health Screening Rewards)
    • OTC, Food & Produce (Over the Counter, Food & Produce)
    • Post Discharge (Post Discharge Incentives)
    • Health Risk Assessment (Health Risk Assessment Rewards)

Android<AdapterViewFlipper> fed by a RemoteViewsService, one item per purse, horizontal slide: inAnimation and outAnimation are objectAnimator XMLs on translationX (in from the right, out to the left), set on the flipper in the widget layout. The fades by the icon and the › are ImageViews with radial gradient shape drawables (tile colour → transparent), placed outside the flipper, under the icon and the ›. The flip control is a view outside the flipper: tap → broadcast → partiallyUpdateAppWidget() with showNext(). Everything else on the tile shares one setOnClickPendingIntent → Care hub › Healthy Spending Card. Two views stacked in the tile: the top half carries the Care hub PendingIntent, the bottom half a fill-in intent → showNext(). "1 of 4" is a static TextView outside the flipper, updated with setTextViewText() in the same partiallyUpdateAppWidget() call as showNext(). The wallet icon is a static ImageView outside the flipper and never changes.

Dropped: not possible on Android widgets

Content peek: widgets have no fade masks or snap-scrolling, so the half-visible next page would land at random scroll positions.

Nudge animation: widgets can't run custom animations. Auto-advance is the closest supported way to show motion.
