Three Humana widgets

Three ways the Humana widget could work, from today's to device-level. Static: today's widget, whose links hand off to the MyHumana sign-in. Flippable card: Healthy Spending Card balances, one at a time. Dynamic: the widget picks the member's next step, and a tap completes it on the device.

Widget 2Flippable card

  • Each purse shows its amount and “1 of 4”, one purse at a time. No total. The “1 of 4” stays put while the purses slide; only the number changes.
  • Tapping the bottom half, with the › in the corner, goes to the next balance: it slides in from the right as the current one slides out to the left. Where the incoming text reaches the wallet icon or the ›, it fades out instead of running under them. After the last purse it wraps to the first.
  • Tapping the top half opens the full list. The wallet icon in the top-right corner stays the same for every purse.
  • Purse names are the Nations portal names, shortened only where they don't fit two lines:
    • Health Screening (2026 Health Screening Rewards)
    • OTC, Food & Produce (Over the Counter, Food & Produce)
    • Post Discharge (Post Discharge Incentives)
    • Health Risk Assessment (Health Risk Assessment Rewards)

Android<AdapterViewFlipper> fed by a RemoteViewsService, one item per purse, horizontal slide: inAnimation and outAnimation are objectAnimator XMLs on translationX (in from the right, out to the left), set on the flipper in the widget layout. The fades by the icon and the › are ImageViews with radial gradient shape drawables (tile colour → transparent), placed outside the flipper, under the icon and the ›. The flip control is a view outside the flipper: tap → broadcast → partiallyUpdateAppWidget() with showNext(). Everything else on the tile shares one setOnClickPendingIntent → Care hub › Healthy Spending Card. Two views stacked in the tile: the top half carries the Care hub PendingIntent, the bottom half a fill-in intent → showNext(). "1 of 4" is a static TextView outside the flipper, updated with setTextViewText() in the same partiallyUpdateAppWidget() call as showNext(). The wallet icon is a static ImageView outside the flipper and never changes.

Widget 1Static, tap-only

  • Today's Humana widget. "Explore your benefits" and "See more" hand off to the MyHumana app, where the member has to sign in first.
  • No balances on the widget: two call tiles, "Call Member Services" and "24/7 Nurse Line".
  • Each tap goes straight to the phone dialer with that line filled in; the member taps Call.

AndroidTwo plain tiles (TextView + ImageView), each with its own setOnClickPendingIntent → Intent.ACTION_DIAL with that line's tel: number (opens the dialer, no call permission needed). No collection widget and no balance data on the widget.

Widget 3Dynamic, device-level

  • Device-level orchestration: the widget picks the member's next step and the tap completes it, with no sign-in hand-off.
  • The top card is personal ("Hi John, it's time for your annual wellness checkup!"). "Schedule an appointment" opens "Let's find you a doctor close to home" (Figma Client-Demos), then the booking flow from the tht-demo-studio appointment scheduling demo: pick a doctor, pick a time, confirm John's details, see the booked visit, done.
  • Once the visit is booked, Care hub lists it under "Upcoming appointments", above "Your benefits".
  • Once booked, the widget's chip reads "View my appointment" and opens Care hub with the visit. Swipe to the second home page: the gear switches widgets, restart starts Widget 3 over.
  • "Powered by Humana Provider Directory" sits between the heading and its text: the list is Humana's own data, and Thrive orchestrates the booking.
  • Below it: "Your Healthy Spending Card" opens the balances in Care hub, and "Member Services" and "24/7 Nurse Line" go straight to the dialer.
  • Laid out as the dynamic widget in Figma ([SB] Assets & Experimentations), keeping only its "Hi John" message, without the Thrive Guide frame.

AndroidThe card is a plain TextView layout the app refreshes with updateAppWidget() whenever the next step changes. Its setOnClickPendingIntent deep-links into the Thrive app's find-a-doctor flow.
